# Access auditor and venue-owner rules — English

This is a **demonstration only**, not a legal opinion, service agreement, certified audit or ownership verification. The App does not operate a real auditor cohort, authenticate owners or process a queue.

## Verification and freshness

Follow the canonical `packages/place/src/types.ts` tiers: 0 (unverified), 1 (reported), 2 (corroborated), 3 (documented field survey), 4 (expiring live status), and E (official-page desk check). These are distinct from prototype labels. This package always writes tier **0**, `PROTOTYPE-UNVERIFIED`, with source name/ID, licence, dates and verifier type on every measurement, including Unknown. It does not collect a `verified` field, accept `VERIFIED`, or issue tier 3. A future genuine tier-3 record needs survey evidence (protocol version, verifier role, method, observed date and photo IDs), reviewed outside this prototype. No evidence block is created here. Tier 4 status must expire at its recorded time and then become Unknown; a date alone never guarantees freshness. Recheck disputed or changed facts and never promote a record merely because time passes.

## Proposed service workflow

1. Receive and time-stamp the correction or dispute; keep the original observation and a full actor/action/time trail.
2. **Proposed target:** acknowledge within **five calendar days**. This is a design target, not a real service commitment.
3. Request and review evidence before changing any verified public fact. Document decisions and actual response times in a future live system.
4. Include unanswered submissions and **misses** (past target without response) in the report. Never hide misses or claim that a demonstration count is a performance record.

## Auditor programme proposal

Define a measurement protocol, safe consent/photo handling, accessible training, conflict-of-interest disclosure, independent review and refresher training before any actual deployment. No training cohort or partner organisation is assumed. Local references are filenames and IDs only; **no image binary leaves the device**. Browser storage is demonstration-only and can be cleared. Use no personal medical histories.
